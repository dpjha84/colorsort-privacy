Privacy Policy – ColorSort Odyssey

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android game ColorSort Odyssey (package com.colorsort.odyssey), published by Divyaprakash Jha ("I", "me"), handles information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (current level, score, stars, booster counts, selected skins and theme, and the player name and avatar you choose) in local storage on your device. This data is not sent to me or to any server, and I do not have access to it. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Accounts

The game has no account or login, and I do not collect your name, email address, or other personal details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details, and ad interaction data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions that require it, you are shown a consent message before personalised ads are used, and you can change your choice later where the consent form allows it.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). More information: How Google uses data from ads and Google Privacy Policy.

Other data

The game does not use analytics, does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files, and I do not sell your data. The app requests internet access (to load ads) and vibration (for haptic feedback).

Children

ColorSort Odyssey is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
